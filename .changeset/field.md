Add the field: a label, an optional description and an error around a control the app brings,
or, with `group`, a fieldset and legend around a group of controls such as pills, radios or
checkboxes. Vue's slot props, React's children function and Blade's `Field::describedBy()` give
the control the ids it points at; `fieldControl()` is exported from Vue and React.
