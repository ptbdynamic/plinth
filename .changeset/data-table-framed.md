Data table: a framed variant (the `framed` prop). From 768px up the table sits in a
`plinth-data-table-frame`, a raised surface with a hairline border and the container radius that
scrolls a wide table, with a header band of small uppercase, letter-spaced column labels, wider
padding at the edges and the actions held to the end of the row. The band's look is the new
`--plinth-data-table-head-*` tokens and the edge padding `--plinth-data-table-frame-edge`.
