Add the checkbox and the switch: a native checkbox (the switch with the switch role), its label
beside it, and an optional description and error under the label, wired like a field's. Both
work with `v-model` (a boolean) and in a plain form; the checkbox can be indeterminate. Adds the
`--plinth-switch-width` and `--plinth-switch-height` tokens.
