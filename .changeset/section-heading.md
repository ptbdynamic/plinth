Add the section heading: the heading of a page section that is not a list, with an optional line
of description and the section's actions, which sit under the text on a phone and on the end edge
from 768px. The heading is an h2 by default (h2 to h6) and can take an id for a
`<section aria-labelledby>`; `divider` draws a hairline under it. Two component tokens,
`--plinth-section-heading-space-before` and `-space-after`, stack sections evenly.
